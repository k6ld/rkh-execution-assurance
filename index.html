Assurance logic

How results are graded

Use this page as a quick guide when interpreting work-order results and required actions.

Current rule versionMAXIMO-MMS-KEYWORD-v1

Green

No obvious discrepancy

The available evidence does not trigger an assurance exception under the current rules.

Typical action: No action

Yellow

Follow-up required

Usually a defect-related or due-date finding that needs engineering follow-up.

Typical action: Engineer review

Amber

Verify on site

Evidence suggests incomplete, pending or otherwise uncertain completion that can be physically checked.

Typical action: Assurance inspection

Red

Serious discrepancy

The work order was closed with evidence of insufficient completion or another serious exception.

Typical action: Engineer / CAR path

Preventive Maintenance (PM)

Common evidence patterns used during PM review

TimingActual Finish is checked against Finish No Later Than.
CompletionIncomplete, partially completed and ongoing activity language requires attention.
Asset evidenceAsset-not-found and generic not-found cases are separated to support the correct action.
Defect referenceDefect wording is evaluated together with the available follow-up reference.

Corrective Maintenance (CM)

Common evidence patterns used during CM review

Pending workWaiting, awaiting, further action and similar future-work language is surfaced.
Temporary stateIsolation, barricading or temporary mitigation is treated as requiring review.
Material constraintsWaiting material, spares and quotation-related language is surfaced.
Access constraintsNo access, MEWP, scaffolding and unable-to-complete wording is treated as a serious exception.

Reading a work-order result

A consistent four-part review pattern

  1. GradeStart with the outcome color.
  2. Matched ruleSee which rule produced the finding.
  3. EvidenceRead the original problem, worklog and keyword hits.
  4. ActionUse the required action as the next assurance step.
